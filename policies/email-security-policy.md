# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Email Security Policy is to establish requirements for the secure use, protection, and management of email systems at BrightCare Medical Group.

This policy is intended to reduce the risks associated with phishing, malware, business email compromise (BEC), unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information, and other email-related cybersecurity threats.

This policy supports the protection of:

- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Financial information
- Confidential organizational data

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Vendors
- Temporary staff
- Interns
- Third-party users

This policy applies to:

- Corporate email accounts
- Cloud-based email platforms
- Mobile email access
- Webmail access
- Email-enabled devices
- Third-party email services used for business purposes

---

# 3. Approved Email Usage

Organizational email systems shall be used primarily for:

- Business communication
- Patient care coordination
- Administrative operations
- Approved collaboration activities
- Authorized communication with vendors and partners

Limited personal use may be permitted if it:

- Does not interfere with operations
- Does not violate policies
- Does not create security risks
- Does not consume excessive resources

---

# 4. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Send sensitive information through unauthorized methods
- Open suspicious email attachments or links
- Use personal email accounts for business purposes
- Forward confidential information to unauthorized recipients
- Send offensive, illegal, or inappropriate content
- Use email for phishing, spam, harassment, or malicious purposes
- Disable security protections or spam filtering mechanisms

---

# 5. Phishing & Suspicious Email Handling

Users must:

- Exercise caution when opening emails from unknown senders
- Verify suspicious requests involving payments, credentials, or sensitive data
- Report phishing emails immediately
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or downloading unknown attachments

Indicators of suspicious emails may include:

- Urgent requests
- Unexpected attachments
- Misspelled domains
- Credential requests
- Financial requests
- Threatening language

Users must report suspicious emails to the Information Security Department immediately.

---

# 6. Sensitive Data & PHI Protection

Protected Health Information (PHI) and sensitive data must:

- Only be shared with authorized recipients
- Be encrypted when transmitted externally where required
- Follow HIPAA requirements
- Be limited to the minimum necessary information

Users must not:

- Send PHI to personal email accounts
- Store sensitive attachments in unauthorized locations
- Share confidential information without authorization

---

# 7. Email Authentication & Security Controls

BrightCare Medical Group shall implement email security controls including:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Spam filtering
- Malware scanning
- Email encryption where appropriate
- Domain authentication technologies

Recommended domain protection technologies include:

- SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
- DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
- DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance)

---

# 8. Password & Authentication Requirements

Access to email systems must comply with organizational password standards.

Users must:

- Use strong passwords
- Protect credentials
- Enable MFA
- Report suspected credential compromise immediately

Passwords must not be shared or reused across unauthorized systems.

---

# 9. Mobile Email Access

Mobile devices accessing organizational email must:

- Use approved security configurations
- Require passcodes or biometric authentication
- Support remote wipe capability where applicable
- Follow the Mobile Device Policy

Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.

---

# 10. Attachments & File Sharing

Users must:

- Scan attachments for suspicious content
- Use approved file-sharing solutions
- Verify recipient addresses before sending sensitive information

Users must not:

- Open suspicious attachments
- Send executable or malicious files
- Use unauthorized file-sharing platforms

---

# 11. Business Email Compromise (BEC) Prevention

To reduce BEC risks:

- Financial requests must be verified through secondary communication methods
- Changes to banking information must be validated
- Credential requests must be treated as suspicious
- Executive impersonation attempts must be reported immediately

Additional verification procedures shall be followed for high-risk requests.

---

# 12. Email Retention & Archiving

Email retention shall comply with:

- HIPAA requirements
- Legal requirements
- Operational requirements
- Organizational retention schedules

Emails containing business records may be archived according to retention policies.

---

# 13. Monitoring & Logging

BrightCare Medical Group reserves the right to monitor:

- Email activity
- Email security events
- Attachment activity
- Spam and phishing attempts
- Unauthorized access attempts

Monitoring is conducted to:

- Protect organizational systems
- Detect security threats
- Support investigations
- Maintain compliance

Users should have limited expectations of privacy when using organizational email systems.

---

# 14. Incident Reporting

Users must immediately report:

- Phishing emails
- Suspicious attachments
- Credential compromise
- Unauthorized email access
- Misdirected emails containing sensitive information
- Malware infections

Prompt reporting helps reduce organizational risk and supports incident response efforts.

---

# 15. Third-Party Email Services

Third-party email services used for business operations must:

- Meet security requirements
- Support encryption where appropriate
- Comply with HIPAA requirements
- Be approved by the Information Security Department

Unauthorized email platforms may not be used for organizational business.

---

# 16. Security Awareness Training

Users shall receive training related to:

- Phishing awareness
- Social engineering attacks
- Email security best practices
- Handling sensitive information
- Incident reporting procedures

Training may include phishing simulations and awareness exercises.

---

# 17. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Removal of email access
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment or contracts
- Legal consequences
- Regulatory penalties

---

# 18. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Organizational security standards
- Email security best practices

All workforce members must comply with this policy.

---

# 19. Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as needed
- Approved by management
- Communicated to all workforce members
