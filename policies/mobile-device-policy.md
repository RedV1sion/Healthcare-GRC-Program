# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Mobile Device Policy is to establish security requirements for the use, management, and protection of mobile devices that access organizational systems, applications, networks, and sensitive data.

This policy is intended to reduce risks associated with lost or stolen devices, unauthorized access, malware, data leakage, and compromise of Protected Health Information (PHI) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Vendors
- Temporary staff
- Third-party users

This policy applies to:

- Organization-owned devices
- Personally owned devices authorized for business use (BYOD)
- Smartphones
- Tablets
- Laptops used as mobile endpoints
- Wearable devices where applicable

This policy applies to any mobile device accessing:

- Email systems
- Cloud applications
- Healthcare systems
- VPN services
- Organizational Wi-Fi
- Internal resources

---

# 3. Approved Mobile Device Usage

Mobile devices may be used for authorized business purposes including:

- Accessing email
- Secure communication
- Accessing approved healthcare applications
- Remote work activities
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Business collaboration

All mobile device usage must comply with organizational security requirements.

---

# 4. Mobile Device Security Requirements

All authorized mobile devices must:

- Be protected with a passcode or biometric authentication
- Automatically lock after inactivity
- Use supported and updated operating systems
- Have encryption enabled where supported
- Use approved antivirus or endpoint protection where applicable
- Have remote wipe capability enabled
- Be registered with the organization if required

Devices failing to meet security requirements may be denied access.

---

# 5. Password & Authentication Requirements

Mobile devices accessing organizational resources must:

- Use strong passcodes or passwords
- Require authentication before access
- Use MFA where applicable

Minimum passcode requirements:

- At least 6 digits for PINs
- Complex passwords for high-risk access
- Biometric authentication only when approved by the organization

Passwords and passcodes must not be shared.

---

# 6. Mobile Device Management (MDM)

The organization may require Mobile Device Management (MDM) solutions for devices accessing organizational resources.

MDM capabilities may include:

- Device enrollment
- Security policy enforcement
- Remote wipe
- Device compliance monitoring
- Application management
- Encryption enforcement
- Access control

Users may be required to enroll devices before access is granted.

---

# 7. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personally owned devices used for business purposes must:

- Follow organizational security requirements
- Use approved applications
- Maintain updated operating systems
- Support remote wipe capabilities where applicable
- Not bypass security controls

The organization reserves the right to remove organizational data from personal devices if security risks are identified.

---

# 8. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Jailbreak or root devices
- Install unauthorized applications
- Disable security controls
- Share devices used for organizational access with unauthorized individuals
- Store PHI in unapproved applications
- Use public file-sharing services without approval
- Circumvent organizational security controls

---

# 9. Application Security

Only approved applications may be used to access organizational resources.

Applications must:

- Be obtained from trusted sources
- Be updated regularly
- Meet organizational security standards

Unauthorized applications may be blocked or removed.

---

# 10. Data Protection

Sensitive organizational data stored or accessed on mobile devices must:

- Be encrypted where possible
- Be accessed only through approved applications
- Not be copied to unauthorized locations
- Not be shared through personal messaging platforms

PHI and sensitive information must be protected in accordance with HIPAA requirements.

---

# 11. Remote Access

Remote access from mobile devices must:

- Use approved VPN or secure access solutions
- Require MFA
- Use encrypted communication channels

Access to sensitive systems may be restricted based on device compliance status.

---

# 12. Wi-Fi & Network Usage

Users should:

- Use trusted Wi-Fi networks
- Avoid unsecured public Wi-Fi
- Use VPN protection when accessing organizational resources remotely

Connecting to unknown or suspicious wireless networks is prohibited when accessing organizational data.

---

# 13. Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately to the Information Security Department or IT Department.

Reported devices may be:

- Locked remotely
- Wiped remotely
- Removed from organizational access systems

Failure to report lost devices promptly may increase organizational risk.

---

# 14. Monitoring & Privacy

The organization reserves the right to monitor:

- Device compliance
- Security posture
- Organizational application usage
- Access activity
- Device inventory information

Monitoring shall be limited to organizational security and operational requirements.

Users should have limited expectations of privacy regarding organizational data and access activities on managed devices.

---

# 15. Incident Reporting

Users must immediately report:

- Lost or stolen devices
- Malware infections
- Suspicious activity
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Data exposure incidents
- Compromised credentials

---

# 16. Device Disposal & Replacement

Before disposal, replacement, or reassignment, mobile devices must:

- Be securely wiped
- Remove organizational accounts and data
- Be inspected for compliance
- Follow approved disposal procedures

---

# 17. Third-Party & Vendor Devices

Vendor or third-party mobile devices accessing organizational systems must:

- Meet organizational security standards
- Use approved access methods
- Follow MFA requirements
- Be authorized and documented

---

# 18. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Organizational security requirements
- Mobile security best practices

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 19. Policy Violations

Violations may result in:

- Removal of device access
- Account suspension
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment or contracts
- Legal or regulatory consequences

---

# 20. Training & Awareness

Users shall receive training related to:

- Mobile security best practices
- Phishing awareness
- Secure remote access
- Data protection
- Incident reporting procedures

---

# 21. Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as needed
- Approved by management
- Communicated to all workforce members
