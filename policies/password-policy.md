# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Password Policy is to establish standards for creating, protecting, and managing passwords and authentication credentials used to access organizational systems, applications, and data.

This policy is designed to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, credential theft, phishing attacks, and compromise of sensitive information, including Protected Health Information (PHI) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Vendors
- Temporary staff
- Interns
- Third-party users

This policy applies to all systems and services including:

- Workstations
- Servers
- Cloud services
- VPN systems
- Email systems
- Mobile devices
- Healthcare applications
- Administrative systems
- Network devices

---

# 3. Password Requirements

## 3.1 Minimum Password Standards

Passwords must:

- Be at least 12 characters long
- Contain a combination of:
  - Uppercase letters
  - Lowercase letters
  - Numbers
  - Special characters
- Be unique and not reused across systems
- Be difficult to guess

---

## 3.2 Prohibited Passwords

Passwords must not include:

- Usernames
- Employee IDs
- Names of family members
- Dates of birth
- Dictionary words only
- Sequential characters
- Repeated characters
- Company name
- Common passwords

Examples of prohibited passwords:

- Password123
- Welcome123
- Summer2025
- Admin123
- CompanyName123

---

# 4. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required for:

- Remote access
- VPN access
- Cloud applications
- Administrative accounts
- Email access
- Privileged systems
- Healthcare applications containing PHI

Approved MFA methods include:

- Authenticator applications
- Hardware tokens
- Push notifications
- Biometric authentication

SMS-based MFA should be avoided when stronger methods are available.

---

# 5. Password Expiration & Rotation

## 5.1 Standard Accounts

Passwords should be changed:

- Every 365 days unless compromise is suspected
- Immediately if compromise is suspected
- After security incidents involving credentials

---

## 5.2 Privileged Accounts

Privileged account passwords must:

- Be changed every 90 days
- Be rotated immediately after personnel changes
- Be stored securely using approved privileged access management solutions

---

# 6. Account Lockout Requirements

Accounts shall automatically lock after:

- 5 failed login attempts

Locked accounts shall remain locked for:

- Minimum 15 minutes
- Or until reset by authorized personnel

This control helps protect against brute-force attacks and password guessing attempts.

---

# 7. Password Storage & Protection

Passwords must:

- Be stored securely using approved encryption or hashing mechanisms
- Never be stored in plain text
- Never be written on sticky notes or visible locations
- Never be shared with others

Users must not:

- Share passwords with coworkers
- Send passwords through email or chat
- Store passwords in unapproved applications
- Reuse passwords across personal and business accounts

---

# 8. Password Managers

The use of approved password managers is strongly encouraged.

Password managers must:

- Use strong encryption
- Require MFA
- Be approved by the Information Security Department

Password managers help:

- Generate strong passwords
- Reduce password reuse
- Improve credential security

---

# 9. Administrative & Service Accounts

Administrative and service accounts must:

- Use unique credentials
- Not be shared among users
- Be monitored and logged
- Follow enhanced password complexity standards

Default passwords on systems and devices must be changed immediately before deployment.

---

# 10. Remote Access Authentication

Remote access systems must:

- Require MFA
- Use encrypted connections
- Use approved authentication systems
- Follow organizational password standards

Access from public or insecure devices is prohibited unless specifically authorized.

---

# 11. Password Reset Procedures

Password reset requests must:

- Verify user identity
- Follow approved verification procedures
- Be logged and documented

Temporary passwords must:

- Be unique
- Expire immediately after first login
- Require immediate password change

---

# 12. Compromised Credentials

Users must immediately report:

- Suspected credential theft
- Phishing attempts
- Unauthorized account access
- Suspicious login notifications

If compromise is suspected:

- Passwords must be changed immediately
- Sessions must be terminated
- Security personnel must be notified
- Additional investigation may be required

---

# 13. Monitoring & Logging

The organization reserves the right to monitor:

- Authentication attempts
- Failed logins
- Password reset activity
- Privileged account usage
- Suspicious authentication behavior

Monitoring is conducted to:

- Detect unauthorized access
- Investigate incidents
- Maintain compliance
- Improve security posture

---

# 14. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- NIST 800-53
- Organizational security standards

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 15. Policy Violations

Violations may result in:

- Account suspension
- Removal of access privileges
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment
- Legal or regulatory consequences

---

# 16. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Creating strong passwords
- Protecting authentication credentials
- Reporting suspicious activity
- Following MFA requirements
- Securing devices used for authentication

---

# 17. Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as needed
- Approved by management
- Communicated to all workforce members
