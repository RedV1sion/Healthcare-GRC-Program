# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Data Retention Policy is to establish requirements for the retention, storage, archival, and secure disposal of organizational data maintained by BrightCare Medical Group.

This policy is intended to:

- Protect sensitive and regulated information
- Support legal and regulatory compliance
- Reduce cybersecurity and privacy risks
- Ensure proper handling of healthcare data
- Support operational and business continuity requirements

This policy applies to all data containing:

- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Financial information
- Employee records
- Business records
- Operational data

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Vendors
- Temporary staff
- Third-party service providers

This policy applies to data stored on:

- Servers
- Workstations
- Cloud platforms
- Mobile devices
- Backup systems
- Email systems
- Databases
- File shares
- Physical documents
- Portable media

---

# 3. Policy Objectives

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Maintain compliance with applicable laws and regulations
- Ensure availability of required business records
- Minimize unnecessary data retention
- Reduce exposure to data breaches and cyber threats
- Support secure disposal practices
- Protect patient privacy and confidentiality

---

# 4. Data Classification

Data shall be classified based on sensitivity and business impact.

## 4.1 Public Data

Information approved for public disclosure.

Examples:

- Public website content
- Marketing materials

---

## 4.2 Internal Data

Information intended for internal organizational use.

Examples:

- Internal procedures
- Operational documentation

---

## 4.3 Confidential Data

Sensitive information requiring protection.

Examples:

- Employee information
- Financial records
- Internal reports

---

## 4.4 Restricted Data

Highly sensitive regulated information.

Examples:

- PHI
- PII
- Medical records
- Authentication credentials
- Security logs

Restricted data requires enhanced protection and handling procedures.

---

# 5. Retention Requirements

Data shall be retained based on:

- Legal requirements
- HIPAA requirements
- Operational needs
- Business continuity requirements
- Contractual obligations

Retention periods shall be defined and reviewed periodically.

---

# 6. Medical & Healthcare Records

Protected Health Information (PHI) and healthcare records shall:

- Be retained according to applicable healthcare regulations
- Be protected against unauthorized access
- Use encryption where appropriate
- Be securely archived when inactive

PHI access must follow HIPAA minimum necessary standards.

---

# 7. Email Retention

Business-related email communications shall:

- Be retained according to organizational requirements
- Be archived where appropriate
- Be protected from unauthorized access

Users must not intentionally delete emails required for legal, operational, or compliance purposes.

---

# 8. Backup Retention

Backup data retention shall:

- Align with operational recovery requirements
- Support disaster recovery needs
- Follow secure storage standards
- Include secure disposal procedures

Backup retention schedules may vary depending on system criticality and business requirements.

---

# 9. Legal Holds

When legal holds are issued:

- Data destruction activities related to affected records must stop immediately
- Relevant records must be preserved
- Authorized personnel must follow legal guidance

Failure to comply with legal hold requirements may result in legal or regulatory consequences.

---

# 10. Secure Storage Requirements

Sensitive data must be stored securely using:

- Encryption
- Access controls
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) where applicable
- Secure backup solutions

Access to sensitive data shall be limited to authorized personnel only.

---

# 11. Data Disposal & Destruction

When data is no longer required, it must be securely destroyed using approved methods.

Approved disposal methods may include:

- Secure deletion
- Cryptographic wiping
- Physical destruction
- Shredding of paper records
- Certified destruction services

Improper disposal of sensitive data is prohibited.

---

# 12. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Providers

Cloud providers and third-party vendors storing organizational data must:

- Meet organizational security requirements
- Support secure retention and deletion processes
- Comply with HIPAA requirements where applicable
- Protect sensitive information appropriately

Third-party data handling practices may be reviewed periodically.

---

# 13. Access Control

Access to retained data shall:

- Follow least privilege principles
- Be approved based on business need
- Be monitored and logged
- Require secure authentication methods

Unauthorized access to retained data is prohibited.

---

# 14. Monitoring & Logging

BrightCare Medical Group reserves the right to monitor:

- Data access activity
- File transfers
- Data deletion activities
- Backup and archive activity
- Unauthorized access attempts

Monitoring is conducted to:

- Protect sensitive information
- Detect security incidents
- Maintain compliance
- Support investigations

---

# 15. Incident Reporting

Users must immediately report:

- Unauthorized disclosure of data
- Lost or stolen devices containing organizational data
- Accidental deletion of critical records
- Suspicious access activity
- Data breaches or exposure incidents

Prompt reporting supports incident response and regulatory compliance.

---

- Protecting organizational data

---

# 17. Security Awareness Training

Workforce members shall receive training related to:

- Data handling procedures
- HIPAA privacy requirements
- Secure disposal practices
- Data classification
- Incident reporting procedures

Training shall occur during onboarding and periodically thereafter.

---

This policy supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- HIPAA Privacy Rule
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Organizational security standards
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 19. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Removal of access privileges
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment or contracts
- Legal consequences
- Regulatory penalties

---

# 20. Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as needed
- Approved by management
- Communicated to all workforce members
