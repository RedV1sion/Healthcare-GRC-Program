# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is to establish acceptable and secure use of organizational information systems, devices, networks, applications, internet services, and data resources.

This policy is intended to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organizational systems and sensitive healthcare information, including Protected Health Information (PHI), Personally Identifiable Information (PII), and other confidential data.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Vendors
- Interns
- Temporary staff
- Third-party users

This policy applies to all organizational technology resources, including:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Mobile devices
- Tablets
- Email systems
- Cloud services
- Wi-Fi networks
- VPN connections
- Servers
- Applications
- Internet access

---

# 3. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Using organizational systems in a secure and professional manner
- Protecting passwords and authentication credentials
- Following organizational security policies and procedures
- Reporting suspicious activity, phishing attempts, or security incidents immediately
- Protecting sensitive and confidential information
- Locking workstations when unattended
- Using only authorized software and hardware
- Maintaining compliance with HIPAA and organizational policies

---

# 4. Acceptable Use

Users are permitted to use organizational systems for authorized business purposes including:

- Patient care operations
- Administrative functions
- Communication and collaboration
- Accessing approved applications and cloud services
- Completing assigned work responsibilities
- Security and compliance activities

Limited personal use may be permitted if it:

- Does not interfere with business operations
- Does not consume excessive resources
- Does not violate organizational policies
- Does not introduce security risks

---

# 5. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

## 5.1 Unauthorized Access

Users must not:

- Access systems or data without authorization
- Attempt to bypass security controls
- Use another user’s credentials
- Share passwords or MFA tokens
- Escalate privileges without approval

---

## 5.2 Malicious Activities

Users must not:

- Install malware or unauthorized software
- Conduct hacking or penetration testing without authorization
- Attempt to disrupt systems or services
- Download malicious files or tools
- Disable antivirus or security software

---

## 5.3 Inappropriate Internet Usage

Users must not:

- Access illegal or offensive content
- Use peer-to-peer file sharing applications
- Download pirated or copyrighted material illegally
- Stream excessive non-business media content
- Use organizational systems for personal business activities

---

## 5.4 Data Handling Violations

Users must not:

- Store PHI or sensitive data on unauthorized devices
- Transfer confidential data to personal email accounts
- Use unauthorized cloud storage services
- Print sensitive information unnecessarily
- Leave confidential documents unattended

---

## 5.5 Removable Media & USB Devices

Users must not:

- Connect unauthorized USB devices
- Use unknown charging stations or devices
- Transfer organizational data to unapproved media
- Circumvent device control protections

Only organization-approved removable media may be used.

---

# 6. Email & Communication Security

Users must:

- Use caution when opening email attachments or links
- Verify suspicious email requests
- Report phishing attempts immediately
- Use organizational email systems for official communication
- Avoid transmitting sensitive information without encryption

Users must not:

- Send PHI through unauthorized channels
- Use personal email for organizational business
- Open suspicious attachments from unknown senders

---

# 7. Password & Authentication Requirements

Users must:

- Create strong passwords
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) where required
- Protect authentication credentials
- Change compromised passwords immediately

Passwords must not be:

- Shared
- Written in visible locations
- Reused across multiple systems

---

# 8. Mobile Device Usage

All mobile devices accessing organizational resources must:

- Be secured with a passcode or biometric authentication
- Use approved security configurations
- Have remote wipe capability enabled where applicable
- Be reported immediately if lost or stolen

---

# 9. Remote Access

Remote access to organizational systems must:

- Use approved VPN or secure access solutions
- Follow MFA requirements
- Be conducted only from authorized devices
- Comply with organizational security standards

Public or unsecured Wi-Fi should be avoided when accessing sensitive systems.

---

# 10. Monitoring & Privacy

The organization reserves the right to monitor:

- Network activity
- Email usage
- Internet usage
- System access logs
- Device activity

Users should have no expectation of personal privacy when using organizational systems.

Monitoring is conducted to:

- Protect organizational assets
- Detect security incidents
- Maintain compliance
- Support investigations

---

# 11. Security Incident Reporting

Users must immediately report:

- Phishing emails
- Lost or stolen devices
- Malware infections
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Suspicious activity
- Data breaches or accidental disclosures

Security incidents should be reported to the Information Security Department or Help Desk immediately.

---

# 12. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Removal of system access
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment
- Legal action
- Financial penalties
- Regulatory reporting obligations

---

# 13. Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in violations of:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- Organizational policies
- Regulatory requirements
- Contractual obligations

All users are required to acknowledge and comply with this policy.

---

# 14. Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as needed
- Approved by management
- Communicated to all workforce members

---

# 15. Acknowledgement

All users must acknowledge that they:

- Have read this policy
- Understand this policy
- Agree to comply with this policy
- Understand the consequences of violations
