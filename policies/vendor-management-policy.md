# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Vendor Management Policy is to establish requirements for evaluating, selecting, monitoring, and managing third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, and business associates that access organizational systems, data, or facilities.

This policy is intended to reduce risks associated with third-party relationships and ensure protection of sensitive organizational information, including Protected Health Information (PHI) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Vendors
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Managed service providers
- Cloud service providers
- Business associates
- Third-party technology providers

This policy applies to vendors that:

- Access organizational systems
- Store or process organizational data
- Provide IT or security services
- Access healthcare systems or PHI
- Connect to organizational networks
- Support operational or business functions

---

# 3. Vendor Risk Management Objectives

The objectives of vendor management are to:

- Reduce third-party cybersecurity risks
- Protect organizational data and systems
- Ensure compliance with HIPAA and regulatory requirements
- Evaluate vendor security practices
- Monitor vendor performance and security posture
- Minimize operational disruptions
- Establish clear security expectations

---

# 4. Vendor Classification

Vendors shall be classified based on risk level and business impact.

## 4.1 Low-Risk Vendors

Examples:

- Office supply vendors
- Non-technical service providers
- Vendors with no system or data access

---

## 4.2 Medium-Risk Vendors

Examples:

- Vendors with limited system access
- Application support providers
- External consultants

---

## 4.3 High-Risk Vendors

Examples:

- Cloud service providers
- Managed IT providers
- Vendors handling PHI
- Vendors with privileged access
- Security service providers

High-risk vendors require enhanced security review and monitoring.

---

# 5. Vendor Security Assessments

Prior to onboarding, vendors may be required to complete security assessments including:

- Security questionnaires
- Compliance reviews
- Risk assessments
- Security documentation reviews
- Contract reviews
- Technical security evaluations

The organization may review:

- Security policies
- Incident response capabilities
- Encryption standards
- Access controls
- Backup procedures
- Regulatory compliance
- Audit reports
- Penetration testing reports

---

# 6. HIPAA & Business Associate Requirements

Vendors handling PHI must:

- Comply with HIPAA requirements
- Sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)
- Maintain appropriate security safeguards
- Report breaches involving PHI promptly

The organization shall verify that business associates implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.

---

# 7. Vendor Access Control

Vendor access to organizational systems shall:

- Be approved and documented
- Follow least privilege principles
- Be restricted to required systems only
- Require MFA where applicable
- Be time-limited when possible

Shared vendor accounts are prohibited unless specifically approved.

---

# 8. Remote Access Requirements

Vendors accessing organizational systems remotely must:

- Use approved VPN or secure access methods
- Use encrypted communications
- Comply with MFA requirements
- Use approved devices where applicable

Vendor remote access sessions may be monitored and logged.

---

# 9. Security Requirements for Vendors

Vendors shall maintain appropriate security controls including:

- Strong authentication controls
- Endpoint protection
- Encryption
- Vulnerability management
- Patch management
- Incident response procedures
- Security awareness training
- Access control measures

The organization may require evidence of implemented security controls.

---

# 10. Vendor Contracts & Agreements

Vendor agreements should include:

- Security requirements
- Confidentiality clauses
- Data protection obligations
- Incident reporting requirements
- Breach notification timelines
- Audit rights
- Compliance obligations
- Service level expectations

Contracts should clearly define vendor responsibilities related to cybersecurity and privacy.

---

# 11. Incident Reporting Requirements

Vendors must immediately report:

- Security incidents affecting organizational data
- Unauthorized access
- Malware infections
- Credential compromise
- Data breaches
- Service disruptions impacting operations

The organization reserves the right to investigate vendor-related security incidents.

---

# 12. Vendor Monitoring & Reviews

Vendor security and performance shall be reviewed periodically based on risk level.

Reviews may include:

- Security assessments
- Compliance reviews
- Access reviews
- Audit documentation
- Performance evaluations
- Incident history reviews

High-risk vendors may require more frequent reviews.

---

# 13. Data Protection Requirements

Vendors handling organizational data must:

- Protect sensitive information
- Use encryption where appropriate
- Limit access to authorized personnel
- Prevent unauthorized disclosure
- Follow secure disposal procedures

PHI and confidential data must be handled according to regulatory and contractual requirements.

---

# 14. Third-Party Cloud Services

Cloud service providers must:

- Meet organizational security standards
- Support encryption
- Maintain availability and backup protections
- Provide appropriate access controls
- Support regulatory compliance requirements

The organization may require cloud providers to provide audit reports such as SOC 2 or equivalent documentation.

---

# 15. Vendor Offboarding

When vendor relationships end:

- Access must be revoked immediately
- Accounts must be disabled
- Credentials must be removed
- Organizational data must be returned or securely destroyed
- Equipment must be returned where applicable

Offboarding procedures shall be documented and verified.

---

# 16. Audit & Compliance

The organization reserves the right to:

- Audit vendor security controls
- Review compliance documentation
- Assess vendor risk posture
- Validate contractual obligations

Vendors may be required to cooperate during audits or investigations.

---

# 17. Monitoring & Logging

Vendor activities may be monitored including:

- Authentication activity
- System access
- Administrative actions
- Data transfers
- Remote access sessions

Monitoring is conducted to protect organizational systems and sensitive data.

---

# 18. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Removal of vendor access
- Contract termination
- Financial penalties
- Legal action
- Regulatory reporting

---

# 19. Compliance

This policy supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- NIST 800-53
- Organizational security standards
- Vendor risk management best practices

All vendors and workforce members involved in vendor management activities must comply with this policy.

---

# 20. Roles & Responsibilities

## 20.1 Information Security Department

Responsible for:

- Vendor security reviews
- Risk assessments
- Security requirement development
- Incident coordination

---

## 20.2 Information Technology Department

Responsible for:

- Managing vendor access
- Technical onboarding and offboarding
- Monitoring technical integrations

---

## 20.3 Procurement & Management

Responsible for:

- Coordinating contracts
- Ensuring vendor documentation completion
- Maintaining vendor inventories

---

# 21. Training & Awareness

Personnel involved in vendor management shall receive training related to:

- Third-party risk management
- Vendor security expectations
- Incident reporting procedures
- Data protection requirements

---

# 22. Review & Maintenance

This policy shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as needed
- Approved by management
- Communicated to applicable personnel
