# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the Security Awareness Program is to educate workforce members at BrightCare Medical Group on cybersecurity risks, privacy responsibilities, and security best practices to reduce the likelihood of security incidents, data breaches, and unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information.

This program is designed to strengthen protection of:

- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Financial information
- Organizational systems and data

The program supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- HIPAA Privacy Rule
- Organizational security policies
- Healthcare cybersecurity best practices

---

# 2. Program Objectives

The objectives of this program are to:

- Increase cybersecurity awareness across the organization
- Reduce phishing and social engineering risks
- Improve employee understanding of HIPAA requirements
- Promote secure handling of sensitive information
- Strengthen incident reporting culture
- Reduce human-related cybersecurity risks
- Improve organizational security posture

---

# 3. Scope

This program applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Vendors with system access
- Temporary staff
- Interns
- Third-party users where applicable

The program applies to all workforce members who access:

- Organizational systems
- Email systems
- Cloud platforms
- Healthcare applications
- PHI or sensitive information

---

# 4. Security Awareness Training Requirements

## 4.1 New Hire Training

All new workforce members must complete security awareness training during onboarding before receiving access to organizational systems.

Training shall include:

- HIPAA awareness
- Password security
- Phishing awareness
- Acceptable use requirements
- Incident reporting procedures
- Data protection responsibilities

---

## 4.2 Annual Refresher Training

All workforce members shall complete annual refresher training covering:

- Cybersecurity threats
- HIPAA compliance updates
- Security policy updates
- Secure remote work practices
- Mobile device security
- Social engineering awareness

Training completion shall be documented and tracked.

---

## 4.3 Role-Based Training

Additional specialized training may be required for:

| Role | Additional Training |
|---|---|
| IT Administrators | Privileged access security |
| Executives | Business Email Compromise (BEC) awareness |
| Clinical Staff | PHI handling and privacy |
| Help Desk Staff | Identity verification procedures |
| Remote Workers | Remote access security |

---

# 5. Security Awareness Topics

The program shall address the following topics:

---

## 5.1 Phishing & Social Engineering

Training shall include:

- Identifying phishing emails
- Recognizing suspicious links and attachments
- Reporting phishing attempts
- Social engineering awareness
- Business Email Compromise (BEC) prevention

---

## 5.2 Password & Authentication Security

Training shall include:

- Strong password creation
- Password reuse risks
- Password manager usage
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Credential protection

---

## 5.3 HIPAA & Data Privacy

Training shall include:

- HIPAA Security Rule awareness
- PHI protection requirements
- Minimum necessary standards
- Secure communication practices
- Data handling procedures

---

## 5.4 Mobile Device Security

Training shall include:

- Device passcodes and encryption
- Lost or stolen device reporting
- Secure remote access
- Public Wi-Fi risks
- Mobile phishing threats

---

## 5.5 Remote Work Security

Training shall include:

- VPN usage
- Secure home network practices
- Remote access protections
- Device security requirements
- Safe remote collaboration practices

---

## 5.6 Physical Security

Training shall include:

- Badge security
- Tailgating awareness
- Visitor management
- Secure workstation practices
- Clean desk expectations

---

## 5.7 Incident Reporting

Training shall include:

- How to report security incidents
- Reporting suspicious emails
- Reporting lost devices
- Escalation procedures
- Importance of timely reporting

---

# 6. Phishing Simulation Program

BrightCare Medical Group shall conduct phishing simulation exercises to:

- Evaluate workforce awareness
- Identify training gaps
- Improve phishing detection skills
- Reduce phishing-related risks

Simulation metrics may include:

- Click rates
- Credential submission attempts
- Reporting rates
- Training completion rates

Users identified as high-risk may receive additional training.

---

# 7. Security Communications

The organization may distribute security awareness materials including:

- Security newsletters
- Phishing alerts
- Security reminders
- Awareness posters
- Email campaigns
- Training videos

Security communications should be distributed regularly to maintain awareness.

---

# 8. Training Delivery Methods

Training may be delivered through:

- Online learning platforms
- In-person sessions
- Video-based training
- Simulated exercises
- Security workshops
- Awareness campaigns

Training methods should support accessibility and workforce participation.

---

# 9. Program Metrics & Reporting

The organization shall track security awareness metrics including:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Training completion rates | Measure participation |
| Phishing simulation results | Evaluate phishing resilience |
| Incident reporting rates | Measure reporting culture |
| Repeat training participation | Identify high-risk users |
| Policy acknowledgment rates | Validate policy awareness |

Results may be reported to management periodically.

- Managing the security awareness program
- Developing training content
- Conducting phishing simulations
- Tracking participation
- Reporting awareness metrics

---

- Supporting onboarding coordination
- Tracking workforce participation

- Protecting organizational information

---

# 11. Non-Compliance

Failure to complete required security awareness training may result in:

- Suspension of system access
- Additional mandatory training
- Disciplinary action
- Escalation to management

---

# 12. Compliance

This program supports compliance with:

- HIPAA Security Rule
- HIPAA Privacy Rule
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Organizational security standards

All workforce members are required to participate in the program.

---

# 13. Program Review & Maintenance

This program shall be:

- Reviewed annually
- Updated as threats evolve
- Revised after major incidents
- Approved by management

Training content should reflect current cybersecurity threats and healthcare industry risks.

---

# 14. Conclusion

Cybersecurity awareness is a critical component of protecting BrightCare Medical Group systems, operations, patients, and sensitive healthcare information.

This program is intended to promote a strong security culture, reduce human-related cybersecurity risks, and improve organizational resilience against evolving cyber threats.
